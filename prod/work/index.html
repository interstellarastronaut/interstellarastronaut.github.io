Tableau Pulse Mobile



Overview

Tableau is a business intelligence software, we help people with their journey to see and understand data.

Tableau Pulse is an AI-powered experience built on Tableau Cloud that delivers personalized, contextual insights directly into your daily workflow. It empowers users to track key metrics, understand why data changes occur, and collaborate with their teams via natural language summaries and automated digests.

In collaboration with directors, product managers, engineering and stakeholders we helped create a product experience that is delightful and modern that still speaks to our Tableau mission.

we help people with their journey to see and understand data.



The Challenge

One of the problems discovered in the development of this product was that our current design system was unable to support a new visual update. The decision was made with design feedback to create ui kit that would support this new product. The Ui kit was designed with accessibility in mind, with future considerations for theming and third party branding for faster implementation. The Ui kit was an update on general tableau components, icons, typography, spacing, elevation and color. I helped develop mobile first principles for mobile and a iteration process to development because one design would not always work over time or over platforms.
